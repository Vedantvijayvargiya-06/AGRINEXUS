AGRINEXUS

Making Horticulture Produce More Profitable than Distress Selling

Slide 2
Proposed Solution / Approach

• Digital Twin Decay Forecaster: Models ambient temperature decay using Q10 kinetics ($TF = 2^{\frac{T-20}{10}}$) to compute live quality % and trigger >70% critical spoilage alerts.

• 4-Pathway Decision Engine: Evaluates Expected Net Income (₹) for Sell Now (Local Mandi), Cold Storage (Holding), Food Processing (Puree/Chips buyback), and Terminal Market Redirection.

• Cluster Freight Pooling (Mandi Connect): Aggregates neighbor harvests in a 15km radius into shared multi-axle trucks, cutting solo freight from ₹4.80/kg to ₹1.85/kg (61.5% savings).

• Fayda Forecast Engine (What-If Simulator): Dynamic sliders to simulate road delays, weather swings, and festival price spikes in real-time.

• AgriNexus Assist (Voice AI): Low-literacy friendly bilingual conversational assistant (Hindi/English) with FPO helpdesk escalation.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Operational Viability, Rural Field Adoption & Risk Mitigation

Slide 4
Feasibility

• Uses proven scientific Q10 kinetic degradation heuristics for reliable decay forecasting.

• Mobile OTP & FPO member records ensure verified and trusted farmer participation.

Viability

• Direct economic incentive: ₹3,000+ freight savings per harvest lot drives active farmer retention.

• Intuitive user interface & transparent math builds deep cooperative credibility.

Practical Implementation

• Ready for pilot rollout in 10 FPOs within 3 to 6 months.

• Zero new hardware requirement at launch; runs on standard smartphones.

Potential Challenges and Risks

01 Connectivity: Patchy rural 2G/3G networks disrupt live updates.

02 Adoption: Farmers hesitate to trust black-box algorithms.

03 Data Privacy: Harvest quantities & payout data require protection.

04 Price Volatility: Mandi prices swing sharply during gluts.

05 Integration: Aligning different FPOs & district transporters.

Strategies For Overcoming Challenges

01 Dual-Mode Sync: Offline local queues + automatic background sync.

02 Voice UX & Audit: Regional voice AI + 100% transparent formula math.

03 Encrypted RBAC: Role-based access control protecting farmer records.

04 What-If Simulator: Dynamic sensitivity sliders to test market shifts.

05 Standard FPO Templates: 1-click cluster confirmation & SMS broadcasts.
